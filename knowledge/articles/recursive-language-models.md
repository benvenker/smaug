Prime Intellect presents a new research direction focused on training models to manage their own context through Recursive Language Models (RLM). This paradigm addresses the challenge of long-horizon agent tasks by implementing context folding - a method for recursively compressing and reshaping an agent's own context.

The core innovation is enabling models to intelligently manage extremely long contexts by treating context management as a learnable problem, rather than a fixed architectural constraint. This approach allows agents to operate over extended interactions without the typical token limitation problems.

## Key Takeaways
- Context management is being treated as a trainable capability for language models
- Recursive language models implement "context folding" to compress and reshape agent context
- This represents a paradigm shift for long-horizon agents in 2026
- The approach enables agents to self-manage context without external intervention
